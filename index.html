Tobacco collection network of central and southern Malawi

Dijkstra's algorithm: shortest road route from a district trading centre to an auction floor. Nothing to install, just choose and click.

Tip: you can also tap two districts on the map. The first tap sets the start and the second tap sets the end.

About this page

The graph has 22 nodes, which are the main trading centres of the districts of the central and southern regions of Malawi, and 27 roads. Each road has its distance in km, measured on Google Maps. The graph is undirected. The three auction floors are the destinations: KA (Chinkhoma floors, Kasungu), LL (Kanengo floors, Lilongwe) and BT (Limbe floors, Blantyre).

How to test it

  1. On the Tobacco network tab, choose a start and an end district and click Find shortest route. You see the distance, the route and the roads used. Below it, a table compares the other possible routes, and another table compares the three auction floors, so you can see why the chosen one is shortest.
  2. Open Show the step-by-step working to see every option Dijkstra checks, including the ones it ignores because they are longer, and the priority queue.
  3. Open Distances from the start district to see the shortest distance to all 22 districts.
  4. On the Try your own graph tab, type any graph and test special cases such as no route, equal routes or a negative distance.

This page uses the same file, dijkstra.js, that is tested by the 33 tests in tests.js. The code is on GitHub: tobacco_collection_system-dijkstra-code.
